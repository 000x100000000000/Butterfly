額外科學知識（一）：完全變態與植物授粉 Extra Science Fact (1): Complete Metamorphosis & Pollination

資料來源：Source: 漁護署《香港物種：蝴蝶》 / AFCD HK Species - Butterflies

蝴蝶屬於完全變態（Complete Metamorphosis）昆蟲，一生經歷四個截然不同的階段：卵（Egg） ➔ 幼蟲（Caterpillar/Larva） ➔ 蛹（Chrysalis/Pupa） ➔ 成蟲（Adult）。

Butterflies undergo Complete Metamorphosis, passing through four distinct life stages: Egg ➔ Larva (Caterpillar) ➔ Pupa (Chrysalis) ➔ Adult (Imago).

  • 專一的食性：蝴蝶幼蟲對「寄主植物（Host Plants）」非常挑剔，例如玉帶鳳蝶幼蟲主要取食芸香科（如柑橘）葉片，而報喜斑粉蝶幼蟲則取食桑寄生科植物。 Host Plant Specificity: Caterpillars only feed on specific host plants. For example, Common Mormon larvae feed on Rutaceae (citrus family), while Red-base Jezebel larvae feed on mistletoes (Loranthaceae).
  • 香港植物的授粉功臣：根據漁護署引述的生態研究（Corlett 2001），香港至少有 6 種本土植物高度依賴蝴蝶傳播花粉，包括玉葉金花（Mussaenda pubescens）、香港大沙葉（Pavetta hongkongensis）、光葉海桐、山油柑、長花厚殼樹及梭羅樹。 Key Pollinators in HK: According to ecological research cited by AFCD (Corlett 2001), at least 6 native flora species in Hong Kong depend on butterflies for pollination: Splash-of-white (Mussaenda pubescens), Hong Kong Pavetta (Pavetta hongkongensis), Glabrous Pittosporum, Acronychia, Long-flowered Ehretia, and Reevesia.

額外科學知識（二）：最佳觀蝶時間與體溫奧秘 Extra Science Fact (2): Best Watching Hours & Thermoregulation

資料來源：Source: 漁護署觀蝶貼士 / AFCD Tips for Observing Butterflies

蝴蝶是變溫動物（Poikilotherm，又稱外溫動物），體溫會隨環境溫度而改變。當氣溫低於 22°C 時，蝴蝶活動能力會大幅下降。

Butterflies are poikilotherms (ectotherms) whose body temperature adjusts according to the environment. They become inactive when the temperature drops below 22°C.

  • 日光浴行為（Sunbathing）：清晨或雨後初晴時，蝴蝶常張開雙翅停棲在陽光充足的葉面上進行「日光浴」以提升體溫，這正是近距離觀察與拍照的最佳時機。 Sunbathing Behavior: In the morning or when the sun comes out after rain, butterflies often bask with wings spread on sunlit leaves to warm up—making it the best moment to observe them closely while they remain still.
  • 最佳賞蝶季節與時段：香港全年平均氣溫約 22.8°C，四季皆有蝴蝶；當中以春夏之交（4月至6月）及秋季（10月至11月）種類與數量最豐富。春夏最佳觀察時段為上午 9:00–11:00 及下午 2:00–4:00；秋冬則為上午 10:00–11:00 及下午 2:00–3:00。 Peak Seasons & Hours: With an average annual temperature of 22.8°C, Hong Kong has butterflies year-round. Peak abundance occurs in spring/summer (April–June) and autumn (October–November). Best watching hours are 9:00–11:00 am & 2:00–4:00 pm in spring/summer, and 10:00–11:00 am & 2:00–3:00 pm in autumn/winter.

法例保護與珍稀物種 Legislative Protection & Rare Species

根據漁護署保育措施資料，本港體型最大的蝴蝶裳鳳蝶（Troides helena）及金裳鳳蝶（Troides aeacus，展翅可達 16 厘米）均屬極具保育價值的物種：

According to AFCD Conservation Measures, Hong Kong's largest butterflies—the Common Birdwing (Troides helena) and Golden Birdwing (Troides aeacus, wingspan up to 16 cm)—are key species of conservation concern:

  • 《野生動物保護條例》（第170章）：嚴禁狩獵、故意干擾、管有或售賣裳鳳蝶。 Wild Animals Protection Ordinance (Cap. 170): Protects the Common Birdwing from hunting, willful disturbance, possession, or sale.
  • 《保護瀕危動植物物種條例》（第586章）：所有裳鳳蝶屬（Troides spp.）均列於附表1，規管其進出口及管有。 Protection of Endangered Species of Animals and Plants Ordinance (Cap. 586): All Birdwings (Troides spp.) are listed under Schedule 1, regulating trade and possession.
  • 寄主植物保護：裳鳳蝶幼蟲的唯一寄主植物印度馬兜鈴（Aristolochia tagala），以及本港最小型鳳蝶「燕鳳蝶（Lamproptera curius）」幼蟲的寄主植物寬藥青藤（Illigera celebica），均受《林區及郊區條例》（第96章）附屬《林務規例》保護。 Host Plant Protection: Both Indian Birthwort (Aristolochia tagala) (host plant of Birdwings) and Illigera (Illigera celebica) (host plant of the White Dragontail, Lamproptera curius) are protected under the Forestry Regulations (Cap. 96).
🏞️

生境就地保育 Preserving Habitats for In-situ Conservation

香港約 40% 土地已劃為郊野公園及特別地區。根據漁護署蝴蝶工作小組評估，高達 99% 的本地蝴蝶物種曾在保護區系統內錄得：

About 40% of Hong Kong's land is designated as Country Parks and Special Areas. Assessments by the AFCD Butterfly Working Group found that around 99% of local butterfly species have been recorded within protected areas:

  • 大埔鳳園谷（SSSI）：於1980年列為「具特殊科學價值地點」，環保協進會透過公私營合作管理協議計劃（獲鄉郊保育資助計劃支持）進行生境管理，區內錄得超過200種蝴蝶。 Fung Yuen Valley (SSSI): Designated as a Site of Special Scientific Interest in 1980 and managed by Environmental Association (TPYEA) under a Management Agreement, home to over 200 butterfly species.
  • 屯門小冷水越冬地：已修復的堆填區，是斑蝶亞科（主要是藍點紫斑蝶及幻紫斑蝶）的重要越冬地點，漁護署曾錄得約 32,000 至 45,000 隻斑蝶在此集體過冬。 Siu Lang Shui Overwintering Site: A restored landfill in Tuen Mun where AFCD recorded 32,000–45,000 overwintering Crows and Tigers (Danainae).
  • 大埔滘自然護理區及烏蛟騰：大埔滘錄得超過 150 種蝴蝶，是森林蝶種的重要棲息地。 Tai Po Kau Nature Reserve & Wu Kau Tang: Over 150 butterfly species have been recorded by AFCD in Tai Po Kau's mature woodlands.
🌱

蝴蝶面臨的威脅與校園保育 Threats & School Butterfly Gardens

城市化發展、生境破碎化、過量使用化學殺蟲劑、外來入侵植物（如薇甘菊）覆蓋原生植物，以及極端氣候熱浪（綠色力量節氣蝴蝶調查指出熱浪會改變蝴蝶羽化周期），均威脅本地蝴蝶生存。

Urbanization, habitat fragmentation, overuse of chemical pesticides, invasive climbers (like Mikania micrantha), and extreme heat waves (Green Power's Solar Terms Butterfly Survey notes heat waves alter emergence patterns) all threaten local butterflies.

  • 建立校園蝴蝶園：透過鳳園蝴蝶園計劃或校園綠化，同時種植成蟲喜愛的「蜜源植物」（如馬利筋、假連翹、五星花）及幼蟲所需的「寄主植物」，在市區建立生態踏腳石。 School Butterfly Gardens: Through programs like the Fung Yuen Butterfly Garden Project, planting both adult nectar plants (e.g., Lantana, Milkweed, Pentas) and larval host plants creates urban ecological stepping stones.
  • 參與公民科學普查：參加綠色力量「蝴蝶普查員」或「全城愛蝶」活動，協助記錄本地蝴蝶分布。 Citizen Science: Join Green Power's "Butterfly Care" and surveyor programs to help monitor local butterfly biodiversity.
